SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS & ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION
Kshetram
Agri-Commerce, Crop Residue Marketplace, and Government Equipment Lending Platform
1. Executive Overview
Kshetram is an integrated agricultural e-commerce and resource management platform designed to
bridge the gap between farmers, certified government procurement agencies, and agricultural input
suppliers. The platform directly addresses crop residue management by creating an economic
incentive for farmers to harvest and sell agricultural stubble rather than burning it, while
simultaneously providing essential farming inputs and daily agronomic guidance.
Core Mission: Transform crop residue from an environmental liability into a verified revenue stream
for farmers, backed by accessible government machinery, quality seeds, livestock nutrition, and daily
actionable farming insights.

3. Core Platform Features
3.1 Crop Stubble Marketplace (Farmer-to-Government)
Enables farmers to monetize harvested crop residue (such as paddy straw, wheat stalks, and
sugarcane trash) by connecting them exclusively with verified institutional and government buyers
(e.g., biomass power plants, bio-CNG facilities, and state procurement boards).

Stubble Listing Creation: Farmers specify crop type, estimated weight (in quintals or metric
tons), moisture level, baled/unbaled status, and farm pickup coordinates.
Verified Buyer Access: Only accounts holding a validated Certified Government Buyer badge
can view contact details, initiate bids, or execute purchase orders.
Pickup & Settlement Tracking: End-to-end order lifecycle tracking from listing approval and
weighbridge verification to direct bank transfer settlement.
3.2 Government Stubble Mower Lending System
Provides small and marginal farmers with affordable, scheduled access to heavy crop-residue
management machinery (such as Happy Seeders, Super SMS, balers, and rotary mulchers) owned
by government custom hiring centers.
Availability Calendar: Real-time visibility into nearby government depots and available stubble
mowers based on the farmer's district or pin code.
Slot-Based Booking: Farmers reserve equipment by acreage and date window with transparent
subsidized rental rates or zero-fee government schemes.
Handover & Return Verification: Digital check-out and check-in logs recorded by the local
equipment officer to ensure timely turnaround during peak harvest windows.
3.3 Daily Farming Suggestion Engine
Delivers exactly one focused, high-impact suggestion per day to every farmer on their home
dashboard and via optional notifications, preventing information overload while encouraging
sustainable practices.
Curated Daily Card: Highlights a single actionable tip covering soil health, irrigation timing, pest
prevention, residue decomposition, or seasonal crop rotation.
Contextual Relevance: Suggestions can be tailored by season (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid) and regional
agro-climatic zones, accompanied by a searchable archive of past daily tips.
3.4 Seed Exchange & Marketplace (Buy & Sell)
A two-way agricultural marketplace where farmers can purchase certified high-yield seeds as well as
list their own harvested, quality-graded seeds for peer-to-peer or institutional sale.
Catalog & Filtering: Browse seeds by crop category, variety, germination rate, harvest season,
and certification status.
Farmer Seed Listings: Allows experienced farmers and seed cooperatives to list surplus clean
seeds with batch details, pricing per kilogram, and minimum order quantities.
3.5 Cattle Feeding Store
A dedicated e-commerce vertical catering to dairy and livestock-owning farmers, offering balanced
nutritional products alongside crop-residue-based fodder options.

6. Key System Workflows & Data Integrity
Double-Booking Prevention on Mowers: Equipment reservation endpoints use database-level
date-range constraints and transactional locks so two farmers cannot book the same stubble
mower for overlapping time slots.
Strict Buyer Gating: API middleware enforces role === 'GOVT_BUYER' &&
verificationStatus === 'APPROVED' on all stubble purchase endpoints, preventing
unverified third parties from accessing farmer stubble listings.
Deterministic Daily Suggestion Delivery: A scheduled midnight background job selects and
caches one active suggestion per agro-climatic zone for the next 24 hours, ensuring every farmer
sees a consistent, single daily recommendation across web and SMS channels.
